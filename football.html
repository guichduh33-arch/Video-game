HOW TO PLAY

Left thumb: touch anywhere on the left side to drop a joystick. Drag to run (directions follow the camera).

Attacking: PASS tap = ground pass, hold = lofted pass. SHOOT hold to charge the power bar, release to shoot — push the stick left/right while shooting to aim and curl. THRU = through ball into space. SPRINT hold to run faster (drains stamina).

Defending: PASS becomes SWITCH (change player). SHOOT becomes TACKLE (tap = standing tackle, with SPRINT held = slide tackle — mistime it and it's a foul). The yellow button becomes PRESS (hold to chase the ball carrier with a teammate).

Skills: swipe on the right side — down = quick turn, left/right = step-over, up = knock-on burst.

First time: press PASS or SHOOT just before the ball reaches you to play it first time (volleys and headers too).

Keyboard: WASD/arrows move, Shift sprint, K pass, J shoot, L thru/press, Z/X/C skills, V camera, Esc pause.
