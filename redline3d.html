3D · CITY SWING
Mouse to look (click the game to lock the mouse). WASD to move, Space to jump.
Hold left click (or Shift) to fire a line at the building wall your crosshair points at (the diamond marks the exact spot). Let go to fly.
Run into a wall to climb it. Slam into enemies fast, or dive (C) onto them.
Q or right click punch · R kick · mix them for combos · X web shot - grabs anything, even people and cars (hold X longer = bigger web wall) · F shockwave · Space in the air flip.
H heal: hold still for a moment and get a heart back (costs 40 charge; getting hit breaks it).
Jump off a tall building for a base jump. Takedowns, tricks and swinging earn XP: every level unlocks a new skill (list below).
Y or Tab (SWITCH button): switch to another Biker Society hero - Redline, Chrome, Volt or Ghost, each with a perk. B first person / third person · T time of day · G graphics quality · P pause · M sound on/off (starts off)
Gunmen, drones, brutes, snipers (watch for the red laser) and jetpack troopers. After 10 takedowns, then every 15, a boss arrives: the blue El Gramaha, then you travel to World 2 for the Sea Eater, and after it the God Sea Eater.
Beat the boss of World 1 and you travel to World 2, the final world: a sea port where the Sea Eater waits. You can also pick a world above.
Sidewalks, parks, rooftops and the road are all safe to land on, and you can run along them. Only enemies, their shots and the bosses cost hearts.
In World 2 the sea never hurts: fall in and you swim (WASD), then leap out with Space, swing from the water, or climb a pier. Your web shot (X) damages the Sea Eater.
On a phone: left thumb moves, right thumb drags to look, hold SWING. Hold KICK for a power kick.
